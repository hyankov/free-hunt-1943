# Changelog

What changed in each release of Free Hunt 1943, newest first. The version you have is shown
under the title in the game (BETA 2026.11.11-10 and so on). Only the newest release can be
downloaded, from the [Releases](../../releases) page.

## Next release

- Every town and village has its own made-up name, in the style of its country (farmland,
  desert or the snowy north). Names are printed on the big map and the minimap, local tasks use
  them, and a building you knock down shows where it stood in the kill feed.
- Towns now differ in size much more. Cities have a dense centre of streets with blocks of flats
  three to five storeys high and a factory quarter with a smoking chimney on the edge; some towns
  have a back street or a small works; villages range from a dozen houses down to a hamlet.
- Villages and smaller towns in green country are surrounded by a patchwork of farm fields:
  wheat, barley, maize, potatoes and beet, cabbages, rape in flower, fresh ploughing and hay,
  most with rows you can see when flying low.
- Bailing out of a plane that is going down, badly hit or out of fuel starts the moment you
  press the button, with no hold to confirm it. Bailing out of a healthy plane still needs the
  one-second hold.
- Holding both rudder buttons (L2 and R2) together works the air brake, and on the ground the
  wheel brakes, the same as D-pad left or right.
- Rockets fly more like the real ones: the motor burns for about a second and a half with a
  bright flame and a thick white trail, then the rocket coasts on, trailing thinner grey smoke,
  and starts to drop gently past about 1 km. It no longer blows up in mid-air at a set distance:
  it goes off when it hits a plane (or passes close to one), the ground or anything on it. A
  miss that gets far away just disappears.
- Every fighter now carries its rockets under its wings, five a side, so you can see how many are
  left (enemies too). They fire one at a time, alternating left and right wing.
- Hidden landmarks to discover: rare, out-of-the-way places scattered through every world, 17
  kinds of them, each with its own made-up name. A crashed fighter or a lost bomber bellied into
  a field, a freighter, an old sailing ship, a submarine or a flying boat run aground on a beach,
  an abandoned lighthouse, a hidden radar station, a secret rocket launch site, a ruined castle
  on a hill, a desert fort, a lost tank column half buried in the sand, a fallen airship, a
  mountain-top observatory, a weather station in the snow, an abandoned mine and an ancient stone
  circle. Each one sits where it fits (ships on the coast, forts in the desert, observatories on
  high peaks). They show as a question mark on the big map and the minimap; to find one, get within
  500 m of it and look straight at it (free look counts), and a banner names it, then the mark turns into a gold star (named on the big map). Found landmarks
  are remembered with your world, and the logbook counts them (LANDMARKS FOUND). They are solid:
  don't fly into them.
- The map icons are redrawn: smooth, rounded pictures with finer detail (tank tracks and
  turret, truck cab, carrier deck markings, house windows, factory skylights, parachute lines)
  and a thin dark outline that stays the same width at any size, so they look sharp on the
  R36S and on a phone instead of blocky. Same icons and colours as before, just cleaner. The
  round minimap has a smooth edge too.
- Lock tone: when the nose is lined up on the target's lead mark, a steady high tone now plays for
  as long as it stays there and stops the moment it slips off, like a missile tone in the movies.
  It replaces the short chirp, follows the sound volume setting and goes quiet in the menu.
- Achievements: 54 named feats to go after, from flying under a bridge, through a desert rock
  arch or down a canyon in the dark, to a loop, a victory roll, a dead-stick landing, sinking a
  ship by flying into it, parachuting onto a carrier or setting off an avalanche. A gold banner
  comes up when you earn one, and they are kept in your logbook between sessions.
- The logbook has two pages now: RECORD (your career, as before) and ACHIEVEMENTS, which lists
  every achievement, the earned ones in gold and the others with how to get them, for ideas of
  what to try. Left/right (or A) switches pages, up/down scrolls; on a phone, tap the page names
  and the top or bottom of the list.
- Parachutes show on the big map and the minimap: a small parachute picture, red for enemy
  crews and green for ours. When you bail out yourself, your yellow plane becomes a yellow
  parachute until you land.

## beta-2026.11.11-11 (11 November 2026)

- Weather works like the time of day in Options - World: WEATHER sets the weather now (clear,
  fair, cloudy, rain, storm, fog), and a separate AUTO WEATHER switch decides whether it stays
  that way (OFF) or changes on its own later (ON). CYCLE TIME is renamed AUTO TIME to match.
- AI planes no longer fly into mountainsides. Bombers and tankers used to fly dead straight and
  climb only gently, so a steep slope ahead got them; fighters looked too short a way ahead and
  only ever pulled up. Now they all look far enough ahead, turn away from anything too steep to
  climb over (dam walls too), and fighters roll upright before pulling out of a dive.
- Trees and cacti are no longer ghosts to your guns: a couple of rounds (one for a cactus) and
  they burst into leaves or green chunks that rain down. A rocket clears the trees round it, and
  flying through them breaks them up too. Saguaros still don't stop the plane, but they burst.
- Desert rock formations come down under fire or when something crashes into them. Tall spires
  topple over and smash along the ground; buttes crumble into their own dust and leave a stump;
  an arch's middle breaks out and drops, leaving two broken horns. Shoot a leg away and the
  middle comes down with it.
- Cannon fire into the ground throws up dust that hangs for a few seconds, so a strafing run
  leaves a trail of dust behind it. Sand throws up the most and keeps it longest.
- The dust a plane lifts flying low and fast over sand or snow is bigger and reaches higher the
  faster you go.
- Water splashes are bigger and much taller (a cannon round about twice as tall as before).

## beta-2026.11.11-10 (11 November 2026)

- The rear-view mirror works: it shows the horizon behind you and any plane sitting on your tail.
- One cockpit for every plane, the fast one. The detailed cockpit cost too much frame rate on
  the R36S, so it and the SIMPLE COCKPIT option are gone.
- Wing roundels are back, and the panel lines, rivets and grime are back on the painted skin.
- Your own hits make the tin "ping"; being hit (by guns or flak) now has its own heavy thump
  and crunch, so you can tell the two apart.
- Enemy fighters that get on your six now close in to gun range instead of firing from too far.
- The big map legend no longer lists WATER.

## beta-2026.11.11-8 (11 November 2026)

- Sandy beaches and rocky shores along the seas and big lakes, with white surf rolling in.
- Volcanic islands far out at sea: cinder cones, crater lakes, black basalt cliffs and dark lava
  beaches.
- Desert dune seas: long wind-shaped dunes with sharp crests and shaded lee sides.

## beta-2026.11.10-7 (10 November 2026)

- The world is kept between sessions: same map and the ground you have already explored.
  NEW WORLD starts a fresh one.
- Rounds hit the actual shape of a plane (fuselage, wings, tail, engines), not a big bubble
  around it.
- Parachute: the left stick steers it the way you point.
- B button: tap to zoom the gunsight (in every view), hold to zoom in on the instrument panel.
- The lead indicator is thin like the rest of the HUD and gives a soft high chirp when your
  nose is on it.
- Rounds and rockets fly at speeds that match the planes; one round in three is a tracer.
- On the runway the brakes hold the plane while the engine winds up. Rolling fast over rough
  ground off the runway damages the plane, for the enemy too.
- Buttons pressed while a menu is open no longer fire when it closes.
- Better frame rate with distant planes, vehicles and towns on screen.

## beta-2026.11.10-6 (10 November 2026)

- The bail-out prompt names the right button for your device and shows how long to hold it.
- Logbook in two columns with bigger text.

## beta-2026.11.10-5 (10 November 2026)

- New reflector gunsight in the cockpit; an amber sight in the outside views.

## beta-2026.11.10-3 (10 November 2026), first public beta

The first public build. What was in it:

**The world**
- An endless world, new every time: green highlands, rivers and lakes, snowy mountains,
  deserts with canyons, mesas, rock arches and oases, open sea with islands and white cliffs.
- Changing weather: clouds, rain, thunderstorms with lightning, morning mist. A full day from
  dawn to moonlit night, with the sun rising in the east.
- Towns and cities, roads, railways with steam trains, convoys, bridges, dams, factories and
  train depots, airfields (some on islands) and aircraft carriers at sea.
- No front line: each side holds pockets of land, and patrols take off from their airfields.

**Flying**
- You fly a Spitfire. Both sides also fly the Mustang, Thunderbolt and Zero, plus bombers and
  tankers, at speeds in proportion to the real planes.
- Arcade controls with automatic banking, cockpit, clear and chase views, free look and padlock.
- Fuel, engine stalls high up, landing and takeoff, a landing aid with approach gates, and
  carrier landings on the arrestor wires.
- Land at any of your airfields to refuel, repair and rearm.
- Bail out, fall, open the chute and steer it down; land safely to get a new plane.
- On Android the phone's tilt can fly the plane (SWAP STICKS), with touch controls and a
  CENTER button.

**Fighting**
- Cannon that overheat, and 10 rockets that drift in the wind and are reloaded only at an
  airfield.
- Enemy fighters that get on your six, a lone veteran Ace, bombers, flak and AA trucks.
  Badly hit enemies try to limp home to land, or bail out.
- Everything can be destroyed: towns burn, bridges drop, dams break and flood the valley,
  rockets into snow slopes set off avalanches.
- Optional local missions in each area (stop a convoy, drop a bridge, break a dam, knock out
  flak, stop a bomber raid, down the Ace, and more). Ignore them if you like: it is a sandbox.
- Damage shows as smoke, a cracked windscreen and malfunctions you can still fly with.

**And**
- A big paper map with fog of war, a round minimap, and a career logbook.
- Menus: START (CONTINUE, NEW WORLD), OPTIONS (VIDEO, SOUND, CONTROLS, WORLD), LOGBOOK, EXIT.
- Positional sound with Doppler fly-bys, engine, wind and warning tones.
